# Skateboard controls

Default keys and controller buttons for the Skateboard addon. Keyboard keys can be rebound in game: press **Q** while riding or carrying the board to open the docs, then the Keybinds page. Keyboard and controller both work, even at once.

## Quick reference

### Riding

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Controller |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Push | W | A |
| Brake (a foot dragged at speed) | S | B |
| Steer left | A | Left stick |
| Steer right | D | Left stick |
| Powerslide (hold) or revert (tap); a foot off in the air | Shift | LT; L3 in the air |
| Pump through a transition | Ctrl | L3, with the left stick held forward |
| Manual (hold); on a rail, onto the tail | C | Right stick held part way back |
| Nose manual (hold); on a rail, onto the nose | B | Right stick held part way forward |
| Hippy jump (hold to crouch, let go) | H | A + B |
| Bail | T | D-pad down |

### Pops

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Controller |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ollie (hold to wind up, let go) | Space | Right stick back, then forward |
| Nollie (hold to wind up, let go) | Z | Right stick forward, then back |
| Kickflip side (held as an ollie or nollie pops) | Alt | Flick the right stick up-left |
| Heelflip side (held as an ollie or nollie pops) | X | Flick the right stick up-right |
| Trick stick (hold, then move the mouse) | Right mouse | Right stick |

### In the air

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Controller |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grab with the front hand (hold) | R | LT in the air; D-pad left on the ground |
| Grab with the back hand (hold) | F | RT |
| Catch, and the dark catch after a flip | Left mouse | R3 |
| Hand plant (hold, off a steep lip) | G | D-pad up |
| Coffin (hold) | Y | D-pad right |

### Board

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Controller |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Get on and off; hold on foot to pick the board up or call it back | E | X |
| Throw the board (carrying it) | Left mouse | RT |
| Board editor: look your board over (carrying it) | I | D-pad up |
| Open and close these docs (riding or carrying the board) | Q | View (Back) |

## Every move

### Getting started

Spawn a board, step on and push off. Everything else builds on that.

**Get on and off**: Walk up to a board and step onto it; step off whenever you like.

- Keyboard: Look at the board and press **E**.
- Controller: Look at the board and press **X**. Press **X** again to step off.

**Open these docs**: These docs open while you ride or carry the board. The board waits while they are open.

- Keyboard: **Q** opens and closes them; so does Esc.
- Controller: **View (Back)** opens and closes them.

**Push**: Kick off the ground with your back foot. Hold it to keep pushing.

- Keyboard: Hold **W**.
- Controller: Hold **A**.

**Brake**: Drag your foot on the ground to slow down. At speed it kicks up smoke off your heel.

- Keyboard: Hold **S**.
- Controller: Hold **B**.

**Steer**: Lean the board to carve. The faster you go, the wider the turn.

- Keyboard: **A** and **D**.
- Controller: **Left stick** left and right.

**Powerslide**: Turn the board across and scrub speed.

- Keyboard: Hold **Shift** at speed.
- Controller: Hold **LT** at speed.

**Revert**: A quick 180 on the ground. You carry on rolling the other way round.

- Keyboard: Tap **Shift** at speed.
- Controller: Tap **LT** at speed.

**Pump**: Crouch going into a curved ramp and stand up in it to gain speed without pushing. It only works where the ground curves.

- Keyboard: Hold **Ctrl** before the curve, let go in it.
- Controller: Hold **L3** with **Left stick** held forward before the curve, let go in it.

**Coffin**: Lie down flat on the board, rolling or in the air.

- Keyboard: Hold **Y**.
- Controller: Hold **D-pad right**.

**Hippy jump**: Jump straight up off the board and let it roll on under you, then land back on it.

- Keyboard: Hold **H** to crouch, let go to jump.
- Controller: Hold **A** and **B** together to crouch, let go to jump.

### Ollie and nollie

Every trick starts with a pop. Pop off the tail for an ollie, off the nose for a nollie.

**Ollie**: Pop the tail, slide your front foot up and level the board in the air. The jump every trick is built on.

- Keyboard: Hold **Space** to crouch, let go to pop.
- Controller: Pull **Right stick** back to crouch, then flick it forward.

**Nollie**: An ollie popped off the nose: the board leaves the ground front first.

- Keyboard: Hold **Z** to crouch, let go to pop.
- Controller: Push **Right stick** forward to crouch, then flick it back.

**No comply**: Step your front foot off and pop the board up with your back foot.

- Keyboard: From a coast, press **W**, then tap **Space** within a third of a second.
- Controller: From a coast, press **A**, then flick **Right stick** within a third of a second.

**Boneless**: Grab the board with your front hand, plant your front foot on the ground and spring up off it.

- Keyboard: Rolling, hold **R** for a third of a second, then press **W**.
- Controller: Rolling, hold D-pad left for a third of a second, then press **A**.

**Fastplant**: Grab the board with your back hand, plant your front foot and spring up off it.

- Keyboard: Rolling, hold **F** for a third of a second, then press **W**.
- Controller: Rolling, hold **RT** for a third of a second, then press **A**.

### Flip tricks

A flip trick is an ollie with a flick: the way you flick decides the trick.

**Kickflip**: The board flips over once along its length, kicked off the toe side.

- Keyboard: **1**, or **Alt** held as you let go of **Space**.
- Controller: Pull **Right stick** back, then flick it up-left.

**Heelflip**: The board flips the other way, kicked off the heel side.

- Keyboard: **3**, or **X** held as you let go of **Space**.
- Controller: Pull **Right stick** back, then flick it up-right.

**Pop shove-it**: The board spins half a turn under you without flipping.

- Keyboard: **2**.
- Controller: Pull **Right stick** back, then flick it straight left.

**Frontside shove-it**: A shove-it spun the other way.

- Keyboard: **0**.
- Controller: Pull **Right stick** back, then flick it straight right.

**360 shove-it**: A full turn of shove-it.

- Keyboard: **-**.
- Controller: Pull **Right stick** back, roll it a little left and back twice, then flick it left.

**Varial kickflip**: A kickflip and a shove-it at once.

- Keyboard: **4**.
- Controller: Pull **Right stick** back, roll it a little left, then flick it up-left.

**Varial heelflip**: A heelflip and a frontside shove-it at once.

- Keyboard: **7**.
- Controller: Pull **Right stick** back, roll it a little right, then flick it up-right.

**Hardflip**: A kickflip and a frontside shove-it at once: the board flips up between your legs.

- Keyboard: **6**.
- Controller: Pull **Right stick** back, roll it a little right, then flick it up-left.

**Inward heelflip**: A heelflip and a shove-it at once.

- Keyboard: **8**.
- Controller: Pull **Right stick** back, roll it a little left, then flick it up-right.

**360 flip**: A kickflip with a full turn of shove-it. Also called a tre flip.

- Keyboard: **5**.
- Controller: Pull **Right stick** back, roll it a little left twice, then flick it up-left.

**Laser flip**: A heelflip with a full frontside turn of shove-it.

- Keyboard: **9**.
- Controller: Pull **Right stick** back, roll it a little right twice, then flick it up-right.

**Late shove-it**: Spin the board under you on the way down, a moment after the pop.

- Keyboard: In the air, tap **Space**.
- Controller: In the air, flick **Right stick**.
- Note: Held instead of tapped, the same key leans the board: see the board adjust below.

**Late kickflip**: Kick the board into a flip on the way down, a moment after the pop.

- Keyboard: In the air, tap **1**.
- Controller: In the air, pull **Right stick** back, then flick it up-left.

**Flip hold**: Keep a kickflip going for one extra turn of the board. It needs a big jump, such as a big kicker or a drop: on flat ground there is not enough air.

- Keyboard: Pop the kickflip with **Space** and **Alt**, and keep **Alt** held through the air.
- Controller: Pull **Right stick** back, flick it up-left, and hold it there through the air.

**Board adjust**: Lean the board under your feet in the air without grabbing it.

- Keyboard: In the air, hold **Space** to lean the board down, or **Z** to lean it up. Tap it instead for a late shove-it.
- Controller: In the air, hold **Right stick** back to lean the board down, or forward to lean it up. Flick it instead for a late shove-it.

**Dark catch**: Catch the board upside down, grip side down, half way through a flip.

- Keyboard: **Left mouse** just after a flip pops, while the board is upside down.
- Controller: **R3** just after a flip pops, while the board is upside down.
- Note: Land on the ground upside down and you step off and run it out.

**Front flip**: Flip your whole body forward with the board in your hand. It needs a lot of air: a drop off a roof, or a big kicker taken fast. Start it the moment you leave the ground; even a moment later is too late.

- Keyboard: As you leave a big drop or kicker, hold **F**, then hold **W**.
- Controller: As you leave a big drop or kicker, hold **RT**, then push **Left stick** forward.

**Back flip**: Flip your whole body backward with the board in your hand. It needs a lot of air: a drop off a roof, or a big kicker taken fast. Start it the moment you leave the ground; even a moment later is too late.

- Keyboard: As you leave a big drop or kicker, hold **F**, then hold **S**.
- Controller: As you leave a big drop or kicker, hold **RT**, then pull **Left stick** back.

### Grabs

Reach down and hold the board in the air. Which grab you get depends on the hand and where you point.

**Indy**: Back hand on the toe edge between your feet.

- Keyboard: **F**.
- Controller: **RT**.

**Melon**: Front hand on the heel edge between your feet.

- Keyboard: **R**.
- Controller: **LT**.

**Mute**: Front hand on the toe edge.

- Keyboard: **R**, pointing toward your toes.
- Controller: **LT** with **Right stick** toward your toes.

**Stalefish**: Back hand reaching behind to the heel edge.

- Keyboard: **F**, pointing toward your toes.
- Controller: **RT** with **Right stick** toward your toes.

**Nose grab**: Front hand on the nose.

- Keyboard: **R** with **C**.
- Controller: **LT** with **Right stick** back.

**Tail grab**: Back hand on the tail.

- Keyboard: **F** with **B**.
- Controller: **RT** with **Right stick** forward.

**Seatbelt**: Front hand reaching across to the tail.

- Keyboard: **R** with **B**.
- Controller: **LT** with **Right stick** forward.

**Nose crail**: Back hand reaching forward to the nose.

- Keyboard: **F** with **C**.
- Controller: **RT** with **Right stick** back.

**Crail**: Both hands toward the nose.

- Keyboard: **R** and **F** together.
- Controller: **LT** and **RT** together.

**Rocket air**: Both hands on the nose, feet on the tail.

- Keyboard: **R** and **F** with **C**.
- Controller: **LT** and **RT** with **Right stick** back.

**Christ air**: Let go of an indy and spread your arms.

- Keyboard: During an indy, press **Left mouse**.
- Controller: During an indy, press **R3**.

**Superman**: Both hands on the board, legs straight out behind.

- Keyboard: Hold **R** and **F**, then press **Left mouse**.
- Controller: Hold **LT** and **RT**, then press **R3**.

**No-foot**: Both feet off the board during a nose grab.

- Keyboard: During a nose grab (**R** with **C**), press **Left mouse**.
- Controller: During a nose grab (**LT** with **Right stick** back), press **R3**.

**One-foot**: Take your back foot off the board in the air.

- Keyboard: In the air, hold **Shift**.
- Controller: In the air, hold **L3**.

**Manual**: Ride on the back wheels with the nose up.

- Keyboard: Hold **C**.
- Controller: Hold **Right stick** part way back. All the way back is an ollie's wind-up instead.

**Nose manual**: Ride on the front wheels with the tail up.

- Keyboard: Hold **B**.
- Controller: Hold **Right stick** part way forward.

### Grinds and slides

Rails and ledges marked as grind rails can be ground. Ollie onto one riding along it to grind, or turn across it to slide.

**50-50**: Both trucks on the rail, riding along it.

- Keyboard: Ollie (**Space**) onto the rail riding along it.
- Controller: Ollie (**Right stick**) onto the rail riding along it.

**5-0**: Grind on the back truck, nose up.

- Keyboard: On the rail, hold **C**.
- Controller: On the rail, hold **Right stick** part way back.

**Nosegrind**: Grind on the front truck, tail up.

- Keyboard: On the rail, hold **B**.
- Controller: On the rail, hold **Right stick** part way forward.

**Boardslide**: The board turned across the rail, nose first, sliding on its middle.

- Keyboard: Ollie at the middle of the rail and, in the air, hold the steer toward it a moment: **A** if it is on your left, **D** if it is on your right.
- Controller: Ollie at the middle of the rail and, in the air, hold **Left stick** toward it a moment.
- Note: Riding fakie it is the other way round. Steer away from the rail for a boardslide.

**Lipslide**: The board turned across the rail the other way, tail first, sliding on its middle.

- Keyboard: Ollie at the middle of the rail and, in the air, hold the steer away from it a moment: **D** if it is on your left, **A** if it is on your right.
- Controller: Ollie at the middle of the rail and, in the air, hold **Left stick** away from it a moment.
- Note: Riding fakie it is the other way round. Steer toward the rail for a lipslide.

**Noseslide**: Sliding on the nose, across the rail.

- Keyboard: Ollie a little to the side of the rail, so only one end of the board reaches it, and in the air hold the steer toward the rail a moment.
- Controller: Ollie a little to the side of the rail and, in the air, hold **Left stick** toward it a moment.
- Note: Riding fakie it is the other way round. Steer away from the rail for a noseslide.

**Tailslide**: Sliding on the tail, across the rail.

- Keyboard: Ollie a little to the side of the rail, so only one end of the board reaches it, and in the air hold the steer away from the rail a moment.
- Controller: Ollie a little to the side of the rail and, in the air, hold **Left stick** away from it a moment.
- Note: Riding fakie it is the other way round. Steer toward the rail for a tailslide.

**Darkslide**: Slide across the rail with the board upside down, grip side down.

- Keyboard: Roll alongside the rail and pop a kickflip with **1**, holding the steer toward the rail from the pop on (**A** if it is on your left, **D** if it is on your right). Tap **Left mouse** a moment after the pop, while the board is upside down and still turning.
- Controller: Roll alongside the rail, pull **Right stick** back and flick it up-left, holding **Left stick** toward the rail from the pop on. Press **R3** a moment after the pop, while the board is upside down and still turning.
- Note: Start the turn as you pop: steering only after the catch is too late, and you land in a 50-50. You stay dark after it: land on the ground like that and you run it out.

**Pop out**: Ollie off the rail, back to the ground.

- Keyboard: On the rail, tap **Space**.
- Controller: On the rail, flick **Right stick**.

**Drop in**: Start from the top of a ramp with the board in your hands and roll straight down it.

- Keyboard: With the board in hand, stand on the ramp's deck facing over the lip and tap **E**.
- Controller: With the board in hand, stand on the ramp's deck facing over the lip and tap **X**.

**Hand plant**: Plant a hand on the lip of a quarter pipe and hold a handstand, then drop back in. It needs all the speed you can push to: ride at the quarter pipe as fast as you can.

- Keyboard: Hold **G** as the board leaves the lip.
- Controller: Hold **D-pad up** as the board leaves the lip.

**Wall ride**: Ride along a wall for a moment: the board turns its wheels to the wall, rides it and drops off.

- Keyboard: Find a kicker beside a wall, angled a little into it. Take a long run-up, pushing hard with **W** several times and right up to the kicker, then ride up it toward the wall and roll straight off the lip. Don't ollie: the pop costs the speed you need.
- Controller: Find a kicker beside a wall, angled a little into it. Take a long run-up, pushing hard with **A** several times and right up to the kicker, then ride up it toward the wall and roll straight off the lip. Don't ollie: the pop costs the speed you need.
- Note: Too slow and it does not stick: a short run-up is not enough.

**Pick up and carry**: Lift the board and walk, run and jump with it in hand.

- Keyboard: Look at the board and hold **E**.
- Controller: Look at the board and hold **X**.

**Throw**: Throw the board ahead of you.

- Keyboard: Carrying it, press **Left mouse**.
- Controller: Carrying it, press **RT**.

**Ride on or set it down**: Put the board down and ride straight on, or just leave it where you stand.

- Keyboard: Tap **E** to throw it down and ride. Hold **E** to set it down.
- Controller: Tap **X** to ride on. Hold **X** to set it down.

**Board editor**: Hold the board up and look it over: it fills the screen, sharp over the game blurred, to turn and zoom.

- Keyboard: Carrying it, press **I**. Drag to turn it, right-drag to move it, scroll to zoom and double-click to put it back; **I** or Esc closes it.
- Controller: Carrying it, press **D-pad up**. The right stick turns it and LT and RT zoom; B closes it.

**Call it back**: Your board shakes, tears loose and flies back to your hand, from up to 2 km away.

- Keyboard: On foot with empty hands, hold **E** looking at nothing in particular.
- Controller: On foot with empty hands, hold **X** looking at nothing in particular.

**Bail**: Land crooked, come down head first or hit something hard, and the skater ragdolls with the board's speed.

- Keyboard: It happens by itself. To bail on purpose, press **T**.
- Controller: It happens by itself. To bail on purpose, press **D-pad down**.
