# Changelog

## 2026-10-21
Title: First Ride
### Added
# Art
Game-ready board - the board is the addon's own model (Assets/models/skateboard_game): blue powered channels, a sticker-bombed underside and 270 community portraits, in three levels of detail (28,538, 12,801 and 4,923 triangles, switching at about 7 m and 17 m) whose trucks steer and wheels spin at every level. It rides on its own measurements (wheelbase 0.501 m, track 0.216 m, wheels 0.0328 m, ride height 0.074 m); the placeholder deck package is gone
Board stickers - the board's 38 stickers never showed and its deck and hardware drew one flat colour: its first set of UVs held only the portraits' and left everything else at one corner of its atlas, where the stickers' alpha test cut them away. Each part now maps with its own UVs
Matte grip - the board's updated model: one continuous black grip (matte, its own texture) in place of the blue strips and segmented pads, only the deck's outer border still glowing, with its stickers, portraits and three levels of detail (28,343, 12,753 and 5,100 triangles)
# Riding
Rideable skateboard - a Sandbox entity (Vehicles) anyone can spawn and get on with Use: their own player is put away (movement, physics body, body, tools; its inventory stays on for the spawn menu), a skater in their outfit (clothes, body groups and skin copied from their body) rides the deck, followed by the skate camera, and Use again steps off beside the board. Getting off waits for the press that got on to be let go
Controls - every skate control on Sandbox's own input actions, for keyboard and mouse and a controller (A push, B brake, left trigger powerslide, left stick click pump, X on and off)
Bails - the skater ragdolls with the board's speed; once the ragdoll comes to rest (six seconds at most) the player is back on foot where it lay, and the board stays where it rolled. A runout (riding side-on into a curb or wall) leaves the player on foot where they stepped off
Drops - a flat landing rides away from about 11 m (Skateboard.HardestLanding 15 m/s); a tipped one bails
Carrying the board - hold Use on the board to pick it up, then walk, run and jump with it: tap Use to throw it down and ride on, hold Use to drop it, left mouse (right trigger) to throw it. On foot with it the skater answers the controls as the Sandbox player does (full speed in about 0.15 s, a stop in about 0.2 s, turns at speed), holds the deck between thumb and fingers and keeps it there, and the use prompt is gone while carrying or riding
Calling the board back - hold Use on foot, pressing nothing, and your board (the one you last rode or carried, or spawned) flies back to your hand from wherever it is: up to 2 km (RecallRange), through anything in the way, about a second from 30 m and 70 m/s from farther; the skater reaches out for it and catches it. It shakes where it lies first (0.6 s, lifting off the ground) and everyone hears it: a sound made in code as it plays (no sound files), a rumble and crackles of static as it shakes, a thunder crack and boom as it tears loose, a deep rising whoosh as it flies and a heavy boom as it lands. Not while riding or carrying, and only your own board (the host checks)
Clear screen - the HUD's hotbar and health are hidden while you ride the board and come back as you get off or carry it (kept hidden every frame of the ride, so the game's own menus cannot bring them back mid-ride); the rest of the HUD stays
Controls card - the board's keys shown top left the way Sandbox shows a tool's (title, description and key glyphs on its gradient, accent and grid): a riding card and a carrying card, for keyboard or controller with the player's own bindings; it shows as you get on or pick the board up, fades after 7 seconds and comes back whenever you stand still
Powerslides that stop - a held powerslide scrubs the board to a stop in about 2 s from a fast push, where it glided on sideways for over 4 s
Controls card - every key as you have it bound, the docs key first; the trick key list is gone from the HUD
Every move on keyboard and controller - every control has a key of its own and is rebindable: nollie (Z), manuals (C/B, tipping a grind onto the tail or nose), eleven trick keys (1 to 0 and -, Z for the nollie version), hippy jump (H), coffin (Y), bail (T), docs (Q). On a controller the triggers grab in the air, the right stick click catches, the D-pad plants a hand, lies in a coffin, grabs on the ground and bails, and View opens the docs. While you ride, the board has the keyboard to itself except voice, chat and the scoreboard
Docs - press Q (View on a controller) while riding or carrying the board for the skateboard docs: a window of its own with a side list of pages, every move with what it is and how to do it on keyboard and controller (each how-to checked in game) and a clip of each, a controller page, and a Keybinds page to rebind every key (conflicts swap; saved; shown live everywhere)
Trick keys - hold Q, 1, 2, 3 or 4 to wind up and let go to pop a kickflip, shove-it, heelflip, varial flip or 360 flip, with no mouse flick (the varial's and the 360's spin is rolled in for you); held longer pops higher, as Space does. While you ride, Q and the number keys belong to the board: the spawn menu and the inventory's slots are left alone until you get off. The keys show small in the bottom left corner, where the hotbar was, while you ride on keyboard and mouse
One trick at a time - a trick plays out, landing and all, before the next can pop: a trick key, Space, the mouse or the stick pressed during a landing waits for it to end
Brake smoke - braking hard at speed kicks puffs of smoke off the heel of the dragging shoe, more the faster you go (from 2.5 m/s, most from 7): white off the shoe, greying as they drift up, spread and fade behind you in about a second. Made in code (the addon ships no texture for it) and shown on every client from how each rider moves, so nothing is sent over the network
Flick tricks let go - with right mouse held, the trick stick springs back to centre once the mouse rests, as a thumbstick let go does; only a windup pulled to its edge holds. It used to stay wherever the mouse stopped, so after a trick the board could sit in a manual on one axle and swing round when you steered (30 degrees a second per m/s against 6); now it turns as it does with the button up
# Networking
Riding in multiplayer - the host decides who rides; the rider's client owns and simulates the board (no input round trip, no cost to the host) and keeps it until it comes to rest; if that player leaves the host takes it back. Every client dresses every rider in its player's outfit, hides that player's body and sees every bail
# Performance
Sleeping boards, distance posing, map gathering of only nearby shapes, faster edge welding and optimized local builds: a sleeping board costs nothing, a ridden one about 1.3 ms per physics step on a dense level, another player's rider about 0.14 ms a frame up close and less with distance
# Animation
The skater's clips load as the board appears, not as someone gets on, and the skater is never drawn in its bind pose
Bonelesses and fastplants - holding a grab (R or F) and pushing off now plays the foot plant: the skater plants a foot on the ground and springs up with the board, instead of the board hopping straight into a grab
No comply - push, then pop within a third of a second: the skater plants the front foot beside the board and pops off it, instead of playing an ollie
Slides follow your turn - landing centred on a rail, steering toward it gives a boardslide and away from it a lipslide; landing a little to the side, toward it gives a noseslide and away a tailslide (the other way round riding fakie), every time. The name used to depend on a few degrees at lock-on, and a noseslide always came out as a noseblunt. The docs' grinds page shows all four
Smooth board adjusts and grabs - letting the trick stick go in the air no longer straightens a leg in a frame, and a board adjust or late flip no longer starts with a jump of the arms
Board adjusts on Space - in the air, hold Space (or Z) to lean the board down (up) and let go for nothing more; tap it for a late shove-it. Holding it used to end every board adjust in a late shove-it
Late flicks play once - one flick in the air plays one late trick and two flicks two; a late kickflip no longer plays a second kickflip behind it
Getting on - the player's body stays shown until the skater's appears (for 0.2 s neither showed), and the skater stands where the player stood and steps onto the board (from further than a step it walks up first), easing from the player's pose, instead of appearing on the board
Getting off - Use steps off with the skater's own step-off. The player comes back where the skater stands, facing its way and standing still, its body easing from the skater's last pose over 0.3 s: it came back facing the world's north (a 60-180 degree turn in a frame) and its body jumped 3-80 in. A throw or a drop hands back once its motion has played out, and a bail once the skater has got up
Smooth clip changes - every bone keeps the speed it had through a change of animation and settles onto the new one, so an arm swinging up through a pop no longer stops in a frame as a flip or shove-it leaves the ground (the board still plays its last clip out to the catch)
One-shot animations hold - a move that outlasts its animation holds the animation's last pose instead of starting it over (a held hippy-jump crouch no longer bobs)
Hippy jump height - the rider no longer flies a body length up (the jump's flight and the animation's own rise were added together); a quick crouch hops up upright and a full one leaps into the hurdle's lean
Smooth steering - the skater leans into a turn and swaps sides without a jerk
Running into a kicker on foot - running at a kicker with the board in hand no longer throws you 10 ft up its curve; leaving a ramp's top edge still launches
Scripted input between physics steps - the docs clips' scripted controls reach the skater's animation every frame, not only on physics steps (a scripted grab let go and grabbed again in the air); real controls were never affected
Hand plant let go smoothly - the board drops back in from a hand plant turning nose down over a quarter second instead of flipping round in one frame, and the skater turns with it; the drop lands where it did
Rolling back down a slope - the skater no longer jumps sideways as the board turns round and rolls back (or as you push off from fakie)
Tricks that land - the board no longer spins back after a shove-it, varial, hardflip or inward heelflip (it turned its whole half spin back in the air and landed up to 60 degrees round): it lands the way it was caught, flips are caught all the way round before you land, and a wind-up held diagonally no longer flickers between two wind-ups
Braking foot on the ground - slowing down, the dragging foot now touches the ground instead of hanging about 3 in over it
Smoother everywhere - the whole pose settles after every foot and hand fit, so the last small snaps and jitters ease over (the worst in a riding session fell from 27.7 to 3.3)
Standing idle - standing with the board, the skater no longer repeats the same 13 s: a 39 s idle generated from it with GenMM
Flick tricks - the controls card's "Trick stick" (hold right mouse, then flick) is now "Flick tricks", which says what it does
Feet on the deck - the skater's shoes stand on the deck instead of floating up to 2 in over it, and lie flat on it over the tail
Smooth motion - every bone of the skater carries on from what was shown, its speed changing by no more than a budget in a frame: clip changes and pose jumps ease over instead of snapping
Stock walk - on foot the skater's hips and legs walk, run, start, stop and jump with the Citizen's own walk while its arms keep the board; on the board nothing changes
Grabs that hold - a hand that reaches the board in a grab (R, F or both) takes hold of the deck where it is and stays on it, turning with the board, until just before the landing; fingers no longer sink into the deck
Planted feet - feet stay where they stand on the deck instead of sliding back into each clip's stance at an ollie's wind-up and after landings
Catching the called board - as the board flies back, the carrying hand reaches out for it, open, and closes on it, and the board stays in the hand (it could float beside it after a call)
Flips and landings - the deck drops away from the feet as it flips and the feet lift clear of it, and a board adjust held through a landing settles under the feet in about 0.3 s instead of swinging round in 0.15 s
